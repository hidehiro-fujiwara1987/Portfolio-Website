ABOUT

I'm Hidehiro Fujiwara, born in 1987 — a sound designer and electronic musician based in Ebetsu, Hokkaido, Japan.

I build my own electronic music instruments using Max 8, creating synthesizers, drum machines, sequencers, samplers, and interactive controllers.

Areas of interest: acid house, experimental electronic music, DIY instruments, creative coding, and live improvisation.

WORKS

Sound Ping-pong 2022

A networked, participatory sound installation presented at Xenakis Networked Performance Marathon 2022 (XNPM22).

XNPM22 was a performance marathon dedicated to the composer Iannis Xenakis, hosted online from the Athens Conservatoire in Greece. Sixteen works from ten countries took part over the network across roughly eight hours.

Multimedia Live Performance for Bass, Electronics and Video 2021

Presented as etoz at Sonicit Concert 2021. A live performance combining electric upright bass with electronics and video created in Max / Jitter.

  • Instruments built from scratch
  • No commercial plugins
  • No samples
  • Everything created in Max
  • Experimental electronic music
